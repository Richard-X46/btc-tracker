Bitcoin On-Chain Dashboard

Blocks and transactions: AWS Public Blockchain Registry, synced in incremental batches every 10 min on Databricks.

Bitcoin on-chain data is activity recorded directly on the blockchain: every block mined, every transaction, and the fee paid per byte of block space. When blocks fill up, fees (measured in sat/vbyte) rise, so block fullness and the fee market are a live read on network demand. Large transfers (> 1 BTC out, shown below as "whales") often precede volatility. Price candles come from the exchange market (Coinbase); everything else comes from the Bitcoin blockchain itself, synced into Databricks.
